Timely Medication Reminders for Every Citizen.

Never miss your medicine. The Smart Medicine Reminder Portal (SMRP) ensures patients, seniors, and caregivers receive automated, verified SMS & Email alerts exactly when medicines are due.

Autonomous Event Scheduling

Multi-Channel SMS & Email
